Teacher notes & challenge ideas

Team Relay: Students alternate turns choosing a continent. If incorrect, the team discusses the clue before the next player tries.

Silent Challenge: No verbal communication; teams use gestures or written strategy.

Teacher Challenge: Use Level 3 and no hints. After each placement, ask students to name evidence supporting the fit (fossils, rock formations, or coastline shapes).

Empowered Principles connection: Knowledge, Sound Judgment, Responsibility, Integrity, Win-Win Focus, Passion, Freedom, and Opportunity.

Tip: A puzzle mistake is useful information—try a new strategy and keep going!
